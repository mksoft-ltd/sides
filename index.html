Sides: The News, Every Angle

This site hosts the daily news digest read by the Sides app: how outlets across the political spectrum and the West/East divide frame the same stories, every point traced to a source headline. Only headlines and short feed summaries are ever stored.

  • digest/latest.json — today's digest
  • digest/index.json — every available date with story count and run cost
  • articles/latest.json — the day's article pool (headlines and short summaries only), read by the topic search
  • digest/YYYY-MM-DD.json and runs/YYYY-MM-DD.json — one digest and one cost report per day

Published by Froggy Eye Ltd. Source: github.com/mksoft-ltd/sides.
