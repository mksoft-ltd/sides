# Sides: The News, Every Angle — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 15 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Sides app ("Sides", "the app") handles on iOS, iPadOS and Android, and
why. Sides is published by Froggy Eye Ltd, a company registered in the United Kingdom ("we", "us"). We are the data
controller for the purposes of UK data protection law. Contact: info@froggyeye.com.

## Summary

- Sides has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own.
- We do not run a server that receives information about you, and we never sell information about you.
- The only company that receives information about your device from the app is Google, through its advertising
  software, described below.
- The app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. Ad requests are non-personalised. Google's advertising software
  collects some device and usage information, described below.
- Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

## What the app does

Once a day, our automated system reads the public news feeds of a set of news outlets and publishes a single digest
file comparing how they cover the day's top stories. The comparison is generated automatically by an AI model from
headlines and short summaries. This system works only with public news content. It receives no information about you
or anyone who uses the app.

## Information stored only on your device

The app keeps the following on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else:
- the latest downloaded digest, so it can be read offline;
- your settings, such as the light or dark theme;
- whether you have bought the ad-free option.

Deleting the app deletes this information.

## Downloading the daily digest

To get the day's stories, the app downloads one public file from GitHub Pages
(https://mksoft-ltd.github.io/sides/), a hosting service run by GitHub, Inc. Like any web request, this reveals your
device's IP address and basic technical details (such as the app's network library version) to GitHub. The request
contains no identifier, account or information about what you read. We do not have access to GitHub's server logs.
GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

## Opening articles

When you tap a source, the original article opens in your device's web browser on the outlet's own website. That
website's own privacy policy applies from that point. Sides does not track which articles you open.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

Unless you have bought the ad-free option, Sides shows a banner ad at the bottom of the screen, provided by Google
AdMob (Google Ireland Limited in the UK and EEA, Google LLC elsewhere).
- Every ad request from Sides is **non-personalised**: ads are not chosen on the basis of your past behaviour, and the
  app does not tell Google which stories you read.
- To serve ads, measure them, cap how often they appear and prevent fraud, Google's software collects information
  such as: device identifiers (on Android, the advertising ID and app set ID; on iOS, an identifier specific to our
  apps, not the advertising identifier, which Sides never requests), your approximate location derived from your IP
  address, ad interactions such as views and taps, and diagnostic and performance data.
- Sides does not ask for permission to track you on iOS (App Tracking Transparency), and does not use your data to
  track you across other companies' apps or websites.
- In the UK, the EEA and Switzerland, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears before any ads are
  requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy options, which appears where these rules
  apply.
- Google processes this data under its own policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and
  https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Buying the ad-free option stops the app from requesting ads.

## Purchases

The optional ad-free purchase is processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play under your account with
them. We receive no payment card details, name or email address. The app only learns on your device whether the
purchase was completed, so it can remove the ads. Apple's and Google's privacy policies apply to the purchase.

## Lawful basis (UK GDPR)

Where personal data is processed through the advertising software, the lawful bases are your consent where it is
required (collected through Google's consent form) and our legitimate interests in funding a free app with
non-personalised advertising and in preventing ad fraud.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict and object to the processing of
your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we hold no personal data about
you, requests about advertising data are best made to Google, and you can manage ad consent in the app as described
above. You can contact us at info@froggyeye.com with any question, and we will reply within one month. You also have
the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (https://ico.org.uk).

## Children

Sides is a general news app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal
information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will
help.

## Security

All network traffic from the app uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Information stored on your device is protected by
your device's own security.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at the same address. If
a change materially affects how information is handled, we will also mention it in the app's release notes.
